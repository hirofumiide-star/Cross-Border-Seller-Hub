# Taiwan Group-Buy Broker Portal

Open [the MVP broker portal](../mvp/index.html#broker). This workflow uses a fictional campaign with one tea SKU, one Taiwanese importer, one assigned broker, and confirmed quantities from two organizers. The shipment and all actions stay in the browser. The two views simulate role-based work; production authentication and notifications require a shared backend.

## Run the mock campaign

1. In the **Seller Hub** view, record the product and document review with the Taiwanese importer and broker. Record that the importer has authorized the broker. The demo allows handoff only after these steps and once confirmed quantities meet the campaign threshold.
2. Hand off the case. Switch to the **Taiwanese broker** view, accept it, and download the generated JSON bundle to inspect its commercial invoice draft, packing list draft, and product label draft. All prices and packing details are fictional.
3. Request a missing document. Switch to **Seller Hub** to log the supplemental material, then switch back to the **Taiwanese broker** view to log the filing. Try the optional sampling or hold path. A document request or hold returns to the previous stage when resolved.
4. Record both satisfactory food inspection and customs release. Switch to **Seller Hub** to record handoff to the receiving hub. Browser history persists across reloads. In the Seller Hub view, confirm final delivery to the buyers in Taiwan as a separate event. The demo records this for all confirmed units at once; the live service closes each order against its own delivery record.

## Operating-model review

| Area | Decision and remaining work |
| --- | --- |
| Channel and seller of record | Start with organizer-led recruitment and a Taiwanese seller responsible for checkout, refunds and imports. A Shopify site can introduce the product. Identify the retailer's confirmed order system before automating the handoff. Keep the separate US parcel consolidation sandbox out of this commercial bulk shipment workflow. |
| Source data and campaign threshold | The [Shopify test order export](shopify-test-import.md) is read-only. Its schema lacks stable SKU identifiers, campaign and organizer IDs, and the destination market. Treat imported test orders as analytics until these fields and a reliable payment/refund feed are available. For production, reconcile paid eligible quantities at the cutoff, exclude canceled or refunded lines, and create one shipment revision from the approved set. Recheck stock and quantity changes before dispatch. |
| Ownership and mandate | Seller Hub gathers product records and coordinates the handoff. The registered Taiwanese food importer appoints the broker and retains proof of the mandate. The broker handles customs and food inspection filing. The broker needs only the assigned shipment and its importer and commodity data. A production portal needs authenticated roles, access checks for each case, authorized file exchange, and a record of changes. A role switch in the local demo is a simulation. |
| Import documents, classification, and sampling | Verify Taiwan's commodity code, label, and import documents with the importer and broker before recruiting for a new SKU. Recheck changes to ingredients, manufacturer, origin, or labels. Link the customs and food inspection references to their respective item lines. Consolidation can create one international shipment with several inspection cases. Set aside time and cost for sampling, storage, holds, and return or disposal if clearance fails. [Taiwan FDA inspection rules](https://www.fda.gov.tw/ENG/lawContent.aspx?cid=5094&id=3068) |
| Warehousing and carrier handoff | OPENLOGI is a candidate for Japan-side warehousing and dispatch. Request written confirmation from OPENLOGI on the chosen tea and shipment size, which service carries the commercial shipment, and whether the carrier can arrange food inspection filing and sampling support or hand it to the nominated broker. The published overseas shipping service confirms shipping and document support. Service for food inspection on this route remains to be confirmed. Confirm warehouse lot and expiry handling, original documents, and the rate for exceptions. [OPENLOGI overseas shipping](https://service.openlogi.com/oms/) · [DHL Taiwan broker handoff](https://www.dhl.com/tw-en/home/express/products-and-solutions/products-and-services-overview/customs-services.html) |
| Release versus domestic delivery | Keep food inspection approval, customs release, warehouse receipt, and final delivery separate. Hand the shipment to the Taiwan hub after the broker records clearance. Only the local fulfillment and delivery roles need recipient addresses. Store international and local tracking separately, and handle shortages, exceptions, returns, and refunds before final settlement. |
| Fees and cash exposure | The pilot assumes TWD 55 per delivered, non-refunded set for Seller Hub, including initial product preparation and routine coordination. At 100 eligible sets that is TWD 5,500 of Hub revenue before labor and tools. Quote brokerage, filing, any sampling or storage, and local delivery separately. The Japanese producer bears agreed third-party expenses after campaign qualification and additional customs hold charges by default. Document the scope, cap, overrun approval, and reimbursement flow before launch. Agree separately on expenses for campaigns that never qualify and refund-related costs. Assess the Hub's margin separately from the Taiwanese retailer's contribution and test whether the fee covers the work. |
| Data and analytics | Keep customs paperwork and recipient details out of AI inputs. Use authorized, minimized campaign data and redacted customer feedback for the producer report. Verify the source terms before using merchant data for competitive benchmarking or training. [Shopify API terms](https://www.shopify.com/legal/api-terms) |

## Production acceptance criteria

- Run one paid pilot with a named Taiwanese importer, an agreed broker and carrier route, and written acceptance and pricing for the first product. Verify a normal release and agree on the procedure for a hold or sampling.
- Start with broker updates in the portal and file exchange the broker already accepts. Add an API or CSV feed after validating the fields, permissions, status meanings, and error handling with the partner.
- For live submissions, require immutable shipment IDs and document versions, reconcile both authority references, guard against repeated sends and stale updates, and accept local delivery only after release and warehouse receipt. Bill operating fees on delivered, non-refunded sets.
